Fly · Race · Fight

By Mailleuestudios and iluvmootubes

W / S or up / down arrows: climb and dive
A / D: steer and barrel roll left / right
Mouse or drag: steer in any direction
Space / Shift or the Nitro button: spend a nitro can for 3 seconds of extra speed
Dogfight: A / D turn, up / down arrows pitch, W fire, Q or 1 / 2 switch weapon (photon cannon / torpedo), Ctrl or E lock on
On foot: W A S D move, hold Shift to sprint (15 s, then 10 s cooldown), Q swap blaster / sword, I inventory, arrow keys or mouse to look, Space or click to attack, Z scope
Touch: drag anywhere to steer; use the on-screen buttons for nitro, fire, lock and pause
Esc or P: pause (Sky's the limit)

Infinite: collect crystals, fly through gold rings to reach the next stage, and dodge the island cannons. One hit ends the run.

Race: beat three rival jets to the finish ring. Rings and crystals give you a speed boost.
